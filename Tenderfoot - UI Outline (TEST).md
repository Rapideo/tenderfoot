## Notes / open questions

**1. Adjudication is a mode of Triage, not its own screen.** Reading a ranked list and marking
would-bid/wouldn't is the same motion as triage — the plan of action already flagged this as the
largest single saving available. I modelled it as `VIEW: Adjudication Mode` under Triage Queue:
same card, same reason capture, different data source, plus an `UNCLEAR` verdict and a position
indicator. **This is the call I'd most like checked.**

**2. There is no landing or home screen.** Single-user internal tool; Triage Queue is the front
door. If that is wrong, everything shifts.

**3. `VIEW: Gated` earns its place because of the corpus finding.** The EQR bundle would have
been killed by a stale extracted deadline three weeks early. Filed-not-deleted (§6.2) is only
worth anything if there is a screen where you can *see* what was filed and put it back. Hence
`RESTORE TO QUEUE` and `REPORT BAD EXTRACTION`.

**4. `DEADLINE CONFLICT FLAG` and `MODAL: Field Provenance` are also corpus-driven.** Three
boilerplates, two deadlines, and the portal right where the documents were wrong. Disagreement
between sources is common enough that it needs a first-class display, not an edge case.

**5. `SUPERSEDED FLAG` on documents** — same reason. A bundle can contain three versions of the
same document. The UI has to say which one is being believed.

**6. Cost-to-pursue ingredients are countable from the bundle.** Form count, page limit,
notarization, references — all mechanically extractable, which is what makes the ten-second
human rating possible (§6.3).

**7. Unsure: does Timing deserve a score slot at all now?** Post scope-narrowing it only asks
"is the window survivable," which is nearly a function of the deadline already on the card. It
may be a badge rather than a score. That would take the four-score display down to three, which
changes the most repeated element in the app.

**8. Unsure: how much Entity Browser is needed before the radars work?** I gave it four views,
but the radars may be able to carry the entity relationships themselves, deferring most of this.

**9. Not modelled: notification and email surfaces (§7.9).** Deliberate — they are tripwires
around the app rather than screens in it, and every decision they could capture is better
captured here.

**10. Not modelled: anything management-phase** — pipeline board, submitted/won/lost, ownership,
assignment (§9).
