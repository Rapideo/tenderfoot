01 Two prompts, once each

Both are held for the tab's lifetime only. Close the tab and you will be asked again — that is deliberate, not a bug.

1 Admin secret The 40-character value on .env line 26. A wrong one answers 401 and clears itself, so you get another go rather than a bricked session.
2 Your name or initials Stored as decided_by on every row this session. Two people scoring cannot be merged into one ground truth without knowing whose is whose.

02 The loop

I Interested CHANGED TODAY Opens the discovery step. It no longer decides on its own — pick a channel, then Save & next. This is the slowest path in the product and it is the price of the gate having a measure at all.
P Pass Opens the reason step. Free text, no chips — a reason vocabulary stays parked until a hand-run derives one. Confirm is Pass & next.
U Undo Appends a return to New; it never deletes. Both rows survive and the later one wins. No time limit — it is simply deciding again.
↵ Open the full record Fields, citations and the documents they came from. Use it when the card alone will not settle it.

04 What to look for

Each of these was proven in a browser before you started, so a failure here is a real regression and worth stopping for.

The banner names all three numbers Top of the card: SAMPLE · 100 of 6,893 · SAM.gov · seed gate-2026-09-02. If it is missing, you are on the ordinary queue and measuring something else.
I opens a step rather than deciding The counter must not move until you confirm. If a card advances the moment you press I, an old bundle is cached — hard-reload.
Confirming with nothing picked refuses You should see “Where else would this have reached you? Pick one — ‘Not sure’ counts.” and the card must not advance. Nothing is sent to the server.
Picking a second chip replaces the first Never two lit at once — the column stores one value, and a UI showing two would be lying about what it is going to write.
Back clears the selection Reopen the step and nothing should be pre-selected. A channel carried over from an abandoned card is the quiet way a discovery rate stops being defensible.
The two confirms do not look alike Pass & next is red; Save & next is teal. A rejection must not look like a save at the moment you commit. Both say & next.
No chips on the Pass step The discovery chips must not leak across the mode. Pass is free text only.
U restores the card and the count Press it right after a decision: the counter goes back up and the card returns. Both database rows survive — that is the design, not a leak.
Sampling did not filter the product Open / with no ?sample=1 in another tab: it should show a much larger queue, around 6,920. Sampling selects what you read, never what the product returns.

05 Already logged, and worth stopping for

Don't re-report

Known, and deliberately not fixed yet

  • The decision bar loses its grey band when either step opens. The bundle keeps one band and swaps its contents; ours drops the background. Two CSS declarations, already written up as audit item 10.
  • A bad channel value answers 500, not 400. Unreachable from the screen — the UI can only send the seven.
  • The reason input is 12px where the bundle is 13px. No 13px token exists yet.
Tell me

Shapes worth interrupting for

  • A card you cannot answer the channel question about. That is the vocabulary failing, and it is the single most useful thing this run can find.
  • A deadline or value that reads wrong against the notice itself — extraction is the layer under the gate.
  • Anything that makes you slow down and think about the interface rather than the opportunity. Ten seconds a card is the design target.
  • A conflict shown inline where the two dates look implausible — that display is new and has had little real data through it.

What this run can and cannot conclude

SAM.gov is the only source that has ever been ingested. Indiana IDOA and Indiana EDS read health ok but have never run — there is no adapter for either. So whatever number comes out of this, it is a verdict on the federal market alone, and it has to be said in those words.

It is still worth having. It is the first time discovery has been measurable at all, and the shape of the answer — how often Nowhere wins, and which channel dominates when it does not — tells you what Indiana has to beat.
