I have an existing design — Tenderfoot, an internal tool for a small consulting firm that finds government contracting opportunities. I want **two changes** and I need **everything else preserved exactly**. The current version is the specification a development team is building against, so unintended changes are expensive rather than merely untidy.

### Change 1 — retire the wordmark placeholder. **The logo is already right; only the label is wrong.**

The header lockup today is:

- a **22×22 rounded square** with a 1.5px accent-coloured border, containing an **8×8 accent-filled square** — the mark;
- **`TENDERFOOT`** beside it, IBM Plex Sans 600 at 13.5px with `.16em` letter-spacing;
- and beneath that, an 8px IBM Plex Mono line reading **`WORDMARK — PLACEHOLDER`**.

**Keep the mark. Keep the `TENDERFOOT` wordmark exactly as it is set. Delete the `WORDMARK — PLACEHOLDER` line entirely.**

**Do not redesign the mark or restyle the wordmark.** They are finished; the only thing that was ever provisional is the label announcing them as provisional. **Do not treat the deletion as an invitation to explore alternatives** — I am not looking for options here.

**The one thing that genuinely needs attention** is the composition left behind. The wordmark currently sits in a two-line flex column (`gap: 1px`) against a 22px mark. Removing the second line leaves a single line that will no longer be optically centred against the mark. **Re-balance the lockup so `TENDERFOOT` centres cleanly on the mark** — and change nothing else about the header: same 28px height, same 20px padding, same 9px gap, same `min-width: 150px`.

If you believe the lockup reads better with something occupying that second slot, **tell me rather than adding it.** An empty slot is a decision; filling it is a different one.

### ~~Change 2 — mobile breakpoints~~ — **REMOVED 2026-08-13. Do not add responsive behaviour.**

**This design stays desktop-only, deliberately.** Do not add media queries, do not add breakpoints, do not make anything reflow. **If you find yourself adjusting a layout so it survives a narrow viewport, stop — that is out of scope.**

*(Reasoning is in `PUNCH-LIST.md` item 2 and is not needed to execute this prompt. In short: the design is fluid but has no responsive foundation, and adding one is per-screen design work rather than a setting. Mobile is being served by a separate app against the same data instead.)*

**So this round has exactly one change: Change 1.**

### Do not change — these are decisions, not oversights

**Colour and radius values.** There are 67 colours and 13 corner radii in the design, and they have been extracted into the codebase with an automated check that fails the build if any value drifts. **Do not adjust, harmonise, rename, or "clean up" any colour or radius.** If a new component needs a colour, use an existing one.

**All copy, verbatim.** Labels, buttons, micro-labels, empty states and modal text are specification, and several of them carry an argument rather than describing a feature. These in particular must survive character-for-character:
- `ORDER · AMBIGUITY FIRST`
- `MACHINE SCORES — A READING AID`
- `COST TO PURSUE — FACTS, NOT A SCORE`
- `GATED ITEMS — FILED, NOT DELETED (§6.2)`

**The placeholders that are deliberate.** `DOCUMENT RENDER — PLACEHOLDER` and the on-screen open questions are there **because a decision has not been made yet, and drawing something would invent it.** Do not fill them in. Do not replace them with realistic-looking content.

**Everything relating to scores, machine assessment, and filtering.** Score strips, assessment panels, filter controls and their settings stay exactly as they are. **They are deliberately not active in the first release and are still being built into the product as interface** — a version with them removed would not match what is being built.

**Layout, navigation structure, screen inventory, and typography.** No screens added or removed. No restructuring.

### What I need back

The updated design, plus **a short list of anything you changed that I did not ask for** — including anything you judged necessary to make the two requested changes work. I would rather hear about a five-pixel adjustment than discover it later.
